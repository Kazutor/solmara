SOLMARA

Streets of the Sun Coast

Rico Vega is back in Solmara with empty pockets and a long list of people to pay back. Steal cars, dodge the SCPD, and pull five jobs across six districts — from Palm Heights to the Marina Bay vault.

An original game. No network requests. Progress saves in your browser.

PAUSED

M — mute music & sound  •  Esc — resume

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D / arrows — walk or drive  •  Mouse — aim  •  Click — shoot

E — steal / exit a car  •  Space — handbrake  •  F — QuickFix garage (hold, $150)

Esc / P — pause  •  M — mute

Stealing cars and shooting raise your wanted level. Lay low or visit the QuickFix garage to cool off. Find all 10 Solmara Stars ($150 each) and the health, armor, cash and SMG pickups hidden around the city.

Finish all five missions to complete Rico's story. Progress saves automatically.
